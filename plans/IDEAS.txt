SMALL SCREENS

When a device has a small screen, they will not want to use certain functions.
I think we can disable certain functions in that case.
People are likely going to use their phone for playing the music or practicing; not music-editing.

Also, on small screens (especially vertical screens) the layout should be completely different, as currently (in the non-Zen screen) the buttons are all over the place; make them nice vertical rows or something.

As you'd not see much of the backdrop anyway, we probably want a dedicated fullscreen solution.

We will brainstorm this some time.
